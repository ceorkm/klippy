Legal

Privacy.

Last updated 15 September 2026

What Klippy collects

Nothing, in the sense that word usually means. There is no account, no analytics and no crash reporting, and no server to send any of it to. We do not know that you installed it.

What it stores, and where

Your clipboard history is written to one file inside Klippy's own container on your disk. It never leaves that disk, and deleting the app deletes it.

What it will not record

Password managers mark what they put on the clipboard as concealed. Klippy honours that mark and never records those clips. You can also mark any clip secret yourself, and it stays covered until you ask to see it.

The one request it makes

Link previews are on by default. When you copy a link, Klippy asks that site for its own preview image so the clip has a picture on it. The request goes to the site you copied, carries nothing about you, and is a switch you can turn off in Settings.

Permissions

Klippy runs inside Apple's sandbox with three entitlements: the sandbox itself, outbound network for the preview above, and read and write access to files you pick yourself. There are no others, and you can read them in the source.
